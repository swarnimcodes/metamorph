Delete your Metamorph account

Metamorph is a gym workout tracker at metamorph.smarniw.com, also available as an Android app.

In the app

  1. Open Metamorph and go to the You tab.
  2. Tap Delete my account… at the bottom.
  3. Type your username to confirm, then tap Delete my account.

On the website, without the app

  1. Open metamorph.smarniw.com and sign in with the Google account you used.
  2. Go to the You tab and follow the same steps as above.

Sign in and delete my account

Can't sign in?

If you can no longer sign in, email swarnim335@gmail.com from the email address on the account, with the subject "Delete my Metamorph account", and the account will be deleted.

What happens

Deletion is immediate and permanent. These are removed from our live database:

  • your account and profile (name, email, profile picture link, settings),
  • every workout, exercise and set you logged,
  • your body weight entries and training programs,
  • the link between your Google account and Metamorph, and all of your signed-in sessions.

Gyms you added to the shared directory stay available to other people, but they are no longer linked to you. Metamorph never sees your Google password.

Deleting your account does not delete your Google account. Signing in again later creates a new, empty account.
