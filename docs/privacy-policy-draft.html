Metamorph privacy policy

Effective 30 October 2026. Applies to the Metamorph Android app and the website at metamorph.smarniw.com.

Metamorph is a gym workout tracker run by Swarnim Barapatre, an individual developer. This page explains what it collects, why, and how you can delete it. Questions: swarnim335@gmail.com.

What Metamorph collects

From your Google account, when you sign in: your email address, your name, a link to your profile picture, and Google's identifier for your account. Metamorph never sees your Google password.

What you enter:

  • a username you choose, and your preferences (weight unit, time zone),
  • your workouts: exercises, sets, weights, reps, durations, distances, reps in reserve, and notes,
  • your body weight entries,
  • the gym a workout was at.

Gyms you add (name, area, city, address, and a maps link if you give one) go into a shared directory that every Metamorph user can see. Do not enter a home address there. Your workouts and body weight are visible only to you.

Automatically: to keep you signed in, Metamorph stores a session on your device and a protected record of it on the server, along with the type of browser or device that signed in and when.

What it does not collect: your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or advertising ID. The Android app asks only for permission to use the internet. There is no advertising and no analytics or tracking software in the app.

How it is used

Only to run the app: to sign you in, store your training, and show you your history and progress. It is not sold, rented, or used for advertising.

Who else handles it

  • Google provides the sign-in. It learns that you signed in to Metamorph. See Google's own privacy policy.
  • DigitalOcean hosts the server and database.
  • Cloudflare sits in front of the website and the API to deliver and protect traffic, so it sees connection details such as your IP address.

"Directions" buttons open your maps app or browser. Metamorph does not send your location anywhere. Your data is shared with no one else, except where the law requires it.

How long it is kept, and deleting it

Your data is kept for as long as you have an account. You can delete your account and everything in it at any time, from the app (You tab → Delete my account) or from the website; see how to delete your account. Deletion is immediate and permanent: it removes your data from the live database. Gyms you added to the shared directory stay, no longer linked to you.

Metamorph does not keep backups of its database, so once your account is deleted your data is gone. If that ever changes, this policy will say so, including how long backups are kept.

The application server records errors and warnings only, not a log of what you do in the app. Cloudflare and DigitalOcean keep their own technical logs under their own policies.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Sign-in tokens are stored on your device and are removed when you log out or delete your account.

Children

Metamorph is not directed at children under 13, and you need a Google account to use it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will also be announced in the app.
